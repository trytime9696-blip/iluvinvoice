How to choose a free invoice generator in India

Updated 9 October 2026 · 7 minute read
Quick answer: Choose a tool that supports Indian addresses and currency, editable GST fields, clear PDF export, sequential invoice numbers and saved customer details. It should be fast enough that you will actually use it for every bill.

Spreadsheets work until invoice volume grows, old versions get mixed up or the layout breaks during PDF export. An online invoice generator gives your billing a repeatable structure without requiring accounting software on day one.

Features worth checking

Professional PDF output

Your customer often sees the PDF before anything else. Check mobile readability, alignment, logo quality and whether totals remain clear when an invoice has many line items.

GST-ready fields

If you are registered for GST, look for supplier and customer GSTIN, place of supply, HSN/SAC, taxable value and separate tax totals. Editable fields matter because the tool should support your accountant’s workflow, not guess compliance for you.

Customer and product records

Saving repeat customers, services and products reduces typing errors. It also makes the next invoice much faster than copying an old document.

Invoice status

A simple paid, unpaid and overdue view can prevent invoices from being forgotten. It is more useful than a complex dashboard nobody checks.

Free tool versus paid plan

A free plan is useful for testing the workflow and sending a smaller number of invoices. A paid plan can make sense when you need unlimited invoices, custom branding, premium layouts or deeper payment tracking. Start with the workflow; upgrade only when the saved time is worth more than the fee.

Create an invoice in five steps

  1. Add your business identity and contact information.
  2. Add the customer and billing address.
  3. Enter items, quantity, price and applicable tax.
  4. Review totals, terms and payment instructions.
  5. Export the PDF, send it and record its status.
