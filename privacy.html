What this preview does

The current browser preview stores sample workspace data and any edits in this browser’s local storage. This build does not provide user accounts, a remote database, payment processing, email delivery, identity verification, government GST verification or an uploaded-file service. Do not enter real personal, banking or tax information into this preview.

Production service disclosures to complete

  • Data categories and the purposes and lawful basis for each use.
  • Retention, deletion, backup, security and data-subject request procedures.
  • Names and roles of cloud, analytics, communications and payment processors.
  • Cross-border processing and data-location details, where applicable.
  • Contact details for the privacy/grievance officer and response timelines.
  • Cookie and telemetry practices, including any consent choices.
