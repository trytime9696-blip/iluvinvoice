GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

The things you might want to know first.

Does iluvinvoice.net file GST returns for me?+

No. iluvinvoice.net helps you prepare invoices and track your billing. GST return filing is not included.

Are GST rates and HSN/SAC classifications verified?+

iluvinvoice.net provides editable tax presets and fields. You should confirm current rates and classifications with your tax professional. GSTIN format checks are not government verification.

Can I download a PDF on the Free plan?+

Yes. Free invoices include a visible “Created with iluvinvoice.net” watermark. Pro PDFs are clean and can include your own branding.

Can I cancel a Pro plan?+

Subscription and cancellation terms will be provided at checkout once payment processing is connected. Billing is not enabled in this preview.
