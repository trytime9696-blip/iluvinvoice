09 · FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Is iluvinvoice.net free?

Yes. The Free plan lets you create up to 10 invoices a month. Pro is listed above but can't be purchased yet.

Does it calculate CGST, SGST and IGST automatically?

Yes. Same state as the place of supply gives CGST + SGST; a different state gives IGST. You can also make invoices without GST.

Does the UPI QR confirm payments?

No. It opens the customer's UPI app with your UPI ID, amount and invoice number filled in. You confirm the money arrived and mark it paid.

Does iluvinvoice.net file GST returns or make e-invoices?

No. It helps you prepare invoices and records. GST returns, e-invoice IRN and e-way bills are done on government portals.

Where is my data saved?

Water Plant data is saved to your account on our server. General invoicing data is currently saved in your browser on this device — download a backup from Settings regularly.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Every screen works on mobile, and you can install the app to your home screen.
