Frequently asked questions

Can I track jars for multiple customers?

Yes. Add customers, then record their daily jar quantities in the monthly attendance register.

Can a previous balance appear on the monthly bill?

Yes. Save the customer’s old pending amount and include it in their bill.

Does every water bill have to show GST?

No. GST billing is selected per customer. Check the tax details for each invoice before issuing it.

Can I make just one customer’s invoice?

Yes. The Water Plant section supports individual bills as well as month-end batch billing.
